Our story

We make trading feel like trust.

Stackly began with two friends, forty handmade goods and one rule: nobody should ever feel unsafe buying from a stranger.

Eight years, stacked one promise at a time.

Scroll and watch each chapter settle on top of the last.

2018

A stall, a laptop, a promise

Two friends list 40 handmade goods and promise every buyer a refund if it is not as pictured.

2020

Escrow changes everything

We hold every payment until the parcel arrives. Disputes drop by 80 percent in one quarter.

2023

A thousand shops a week

Tracked shipping and in-app chat open the doors to sellers in 60 countries.

2026

180,000 sellers, one standard

Today 96 countries trade on Stackly, and the promise has not changed.
